So we are watching television. I am not interested in what is on. I have seen it before. But I watch anyway. I am trying to be sociable. The blind man sits on the sofa, his hands folded in his lap. He is a tall man, with a beard and a thick neck. His eyes are covered by dark glasses. I have never known a blind person before. I do not know what to say to him. My wife is in the kitchen, making drinks. She calls out, "Robert, would you like a drink?" The blind man says, "Yes, I would. A little whiskey, if you have it." My wife brings the drinks. She hands the blind man his glass. He takes it, sniffs it, and says, "Ah, good whiskey." He drinks. I watch him. I have never seen a blind man drink before. He does not spill anything. He does not miss his mouth. I am impressed. I think, maybe this will not be so bad after all. But I do not know what to say. My wife sits down beside me. She looks at me. She knows I am uncomfortable. She says, "Honey, why don't you show Robert the cathedral?"
