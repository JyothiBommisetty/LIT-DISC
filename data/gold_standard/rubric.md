# Gold-Standard Evaluation Rubric

## Rating Scale (1–7 Likert)

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Absent | The dimension is not present / irrelevant to this passage |
| 2 | Very Low | Barely perceptible trace; negligible contribution to richness |
| 3 | Low | Present but weak; one-dimensional or stereotyped |
| 4 | Moderate | Clearly present with some variation/nuance; competent |
| 5 | High | Strong signal across multiple indicators; notable literary craft |
| 6 | Very High | Dense, complex, multi-layered expression of this dimension |
| 7 | Exceptional | Extraordinary richness; canonical example of this dimension |

## Axes

| # | Axis | Definition | Key Indicators |
|---|------|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | Semantic (S) | Lexical density, wordplay, polysemy, figurative language richness | Unusual vocabulary, metaphor density, connotation layers, register sophistication |
| 2 | Narrative (N) | Tension curve, pacing variety, scene structure, plot dynamics | Suspense, narrative acceleration/deceleration, turning points, structural surprises |
| 3 | Interaction (I) | Dialogue complexity, speaker dynamics, social interplay | Subtext in conversation, turn-taking patterns, asymmetry, unspoken tension |
| 4 | Symbolic (Sy) | Motifs, symbols, allegorical resonance | Recurring imagery, objects/events carrying extra meaning, thematic coherence |
| 5 | Evolution (E) | Character change, relationship arcs, developmental trajectory | Growth/decay in characters or relationships, insight shifts, perspective changes |
| 6 | Emotion (Em) | Affective range, intensity dynamics, emotional nuance | Valence shifts, emotional complexity (mixed feelings), intensity curve |
| 7 | Overall LDRI | Holistic judgment of literary discourse richness | Integration of all six, cross-dimensional coupling, lasting impression |

## Additional Fields

- **Confidence** (1–5): How sure are you of your ratings for this passage?
- **Notes** (free text): What specific textual features drove your scores?
