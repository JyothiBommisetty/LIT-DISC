I was born in the city of Bombay... once upon a time. No, that won't do, there is no getting away from the date: I was born in Doctor Narlikar's Nursing Home on August 15th, 1947. And the time? The time matters, too. Well then: at night. No, it's important to be more precise... on the stroke of midnight, as a matter of fact. Clock-hands joined palms in respectful greeting as I came. Oh, spell it out, spell it out: at the precise instant of India's arrival at independence, I tumbled forth into the world. There were gasps. And, outside, fireworks and crowds. A few seconds later, my sister was born. She arrived at the very instant of India's birth; but she was not the child of midnight. She was the afterthought, the footnote, the asterisk. I was the one who mattered, the one who was connected to history, who had been chosen by history. My birth was no accident; it was destined. And everything that followed was written in the stars.
