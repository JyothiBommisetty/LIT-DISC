She gave birth to a son, the most beautiful child imaginable. The emperor was overjoyed and immediately sent for the baby to be brought to the palace. The child grew into a handsome youth, and the emperor wished to make him crown prince, but he had no protectors among the great families, and the move would have caused too much opposition. So the child was made a commoner and given the surname Minamoto. He was known as Genji, the Shining One, and he excelled in everything he undertook. He was skilled in poetry and music, in dance and calligraphy, and his beauty was such that all who saw him were captivated.


In a certain reign there was a lady not of the first rank whom the emperor loved more than any of the others. The grand ladies with high ambitions thought her a presumptuous upstart, and lesser ladies were still more resentful. Everything she did was criticized, and she fell gravely ill. The emperor neglected his other consorts to care for her, and their resentment grew. The lady was gentle and retiring by nature, but her sufferings were great.
