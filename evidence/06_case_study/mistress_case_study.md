## Narrative Overview

The novel follows Tilo, a woman trained as a spice mistress on a magical island, who runs a spice shop in Oakland, California. She uses the spices' magical properties to help her customers but must obey strict rules: she cannot touch anyone, leave the store, or use spices for herself. When she falls in love with Raven, a customer, she faces a crisis of duty versus desire, ultimately choosing her humanity over her powers.

## LDRI Evolution Across the Novel

The LDRI trajectory traces the novel's narrative arc:

1. **Opening** (W1–W10): LDRI oscillates, falling from lyrical youth narrative to store exposition
2. **Early crisis** (W11–W20): Rise to W17 (channeling the Old One) then plunge
3. **Middle** (W21–W35): Lower LDRI with spike at W26 (makaradwaj lore)
4. **Spiritual crisis** (W36–W50): Two spikes — W40 (self-punishment, novel peak) and W45 (seeking First Mother)
5. **Action** (W51–W60): W55 assault scene
6. **Memory/trauma** (W61–W75): Low LDRI during introspection
7. **Climax** (W76–W85): W81 reunion → W82 renunciation (tightest peak→valley)
8. **Resolution** (W86–W105): W89 transformation spike, then denouement

## Interpretation

LDRI peaks correspond to scenes of highest literary-discourse intensity: spiritual crisis, violence, emotional reunion, bodily transformation. Valleys correspond to exposition, introspection, and commercial transaction. This validates that LDRI, despite gamma dominance, tracks narrative structure at novel length.

The LDRI values for the novel (mean=0.010) are ~1/3 of the gold-standard passage mean (0.028). This reduction is driven by lower gamma at novel scale, not by reduced feature richness (raw LDRI is comparable at 0.950 vs 0.913).
